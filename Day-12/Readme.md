🚀 BrandForge AI

AI-Powered Personal Branding & Job Search Toolkit

BrandForge AI is a career intelligence project built to help candidates strengthen their professional brand, optimize resumes for ATS systems, discover high-fit roles, and improve interview readiness using AI-driven insights.

✨ Features

Professional Profile Analysis

Summarizes role, experience, skills, and strengths.

ATS Resume Assessment

Evaluates keyword match, resume structure, and recruiter compatibility.

Personal Branding Toolkit

Generates ready-to-use outreach messages, cover letters, and networking templates.

Top Role Recommendations

Identifies the most relevant job titles based on your profile.

Skill Gap Analysis

Highlights current strengths and missing skills with a learning roadmap.

Market Insights

Shows in-demand technologies and hiring trends.

Interview Readiness Assessment

Provides personalized talking points and recruiter expectations.

📌 Project Goal

The project demonstrates how AI can act as a personalized career coach by combining resume optimization, job search strategy, personal branding, and market intelligence into one workflow.
