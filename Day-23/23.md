# Day 23 – Customer & MVP Blueprint

## AI Startup Validation Challenge

### Overview

Today focused on identifying the ideal customer, validating core pain points, and defining the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) for the startup idea.

**Startup:** AI-Agentic Orchestration for MSME Supply Chain Resilience

---

## Executive Summary

The goal is to help Indian MSMEs improve procurement efficiency and supply-chain resilience using AI-powered procurement agents and decision-support systems.

The blueprint maps customer needs, buying behavior, MVP priorities, pricing assumptions, and implementation risks.

## MVP Recommendation

### Build First

* Supplier Recommendation Engine
* Procurement Dashboard
* Inventory Alerts
* AI Procurement Assistant

### Do Not Build Initially

* ERP Replacement System
* Advanced Digital Twins
* Full Workflow Automation

### Success Metrics

* 30% reduction in procurement time
* 20% operational cost savings
* 80% user adoption rate
* 90% supplier matching accuracy
