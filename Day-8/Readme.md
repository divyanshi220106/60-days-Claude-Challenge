# 🌍 Personal Environmental Health Analyzer

## 📌 Overview

The **Personal Environmental Health Analyzer** is an interactive dashboard designed to help users understand how environmental conditions impact their daily health and well-being.

The dashboard combines **Air Quality Index (AQI)** metrics, pollutant analysis, environmental health scores, and personalized recommendations into a single visual platform.

Built as part of my **60 Days AI Challenge – Day 8**, this project demonstrates how AI can be used to transform environmental data into actionable health insights through modern dashboard design.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze environmental health indicators across multiple cities.
* Compare AQI, PM2.5, and PM10 pollution levels.
* Generate environmental health scores.
* Identify cleanest and most polluted cities.
* Provide personalized recommendations for:

  * Respiratory health
  * Skin health
  * Hair health
  * Outdoor activities
* Deliver insights through an interactive and visually appealing dashboard.
