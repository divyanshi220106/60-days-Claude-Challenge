# Day 14 – AI Job Red Flag Detector Challenge 🚩

## Overview

Today I built and tested an AI-powered Job Red Flag Detector using Claude. The goal was to analyze job descriptions and company information to identify hidden risks before applying.

## Objective

Use AI to evaluate:

* Unrealistic job requirements
* Toxic workplace indicators
* Remote job authenticity
* Hiring process risks
* Company stability and reputation

## Steps Performed

1. Gathered Job Description (JD)
2. Collected Company Information
3. Used the AI Job Red Flag Detector Prompt
4. Generated a complete Risk Analysis Report
5. Reviewed Overall Risk Score
6. Analyzed Red Flags and Positive Signals
7. Evaluated Risk Breakdown
8. Reviewed Final Verdict
9. Examined AI-generated Interview Questions
10. Captured screenshots of the results

## Key Features of the Detector

### Overall Risk Score

Provides a score between 0-100 indicating potential job risk.

### Red Flag Detection

Identifies:

* Excessive experience requirements
* Unrealistic skill expectations
* Burnout culture indicators
* Vague responsibilities
* Missing salary information

### Positive Signal Detection

Highlights:

* Growth opportunities
* Learning environment
* Clear role expectations
* Strong company fundamentals

### Smart Interview Questions

Generates targeted questions to validate concerns during interviews.

## Key Learnings

* Not every attractive job posting is a good opportunity.
* AI can quickly identify hidden hiring risks.
* Salary transparency is an important indicator.
* Interview questions should be used to validate assumptions.
* Company research is as important as resume preparation.

## Tools Used

* Claude AI
* Job Description Data
* Company Information Sources
* GitHub

## Outcome

Successfully generated a complete AI-powered job risk analysis report and learned how to evaluate opportunities more strategically before applying.
