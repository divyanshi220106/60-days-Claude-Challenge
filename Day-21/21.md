# Day 21 – Digital Privacy Dashboard | #60DaysOfClaudeChallenge

## Overview

Today I built a Digital Privacy Dashboard using Claude AI. The goal was to analyze a user's digital footprint based on publicly provided service usage data and transform it into a visually rich cybersecurity-style analytics dashboard.

The dashboard evaluates digital exposure, privacy posture, ecosystem concentration, and potential data collection patterns while clearly separating Facts from Estimates.

## Features Implemented

### Digital Footprint Score

* Calculates overall digital exposure
* Categorizes footprint as Minimal, Moderate, Significant, or Extensive

### Privacy Score

* Evaluates estimated privacy posture
* Highlights potential privacy strengths and weaknesses

### Exposure Heatmap

* Visual representation of exposure across:

  * Communication
  * Social Media
  * Shopping
  * Search
  * Payments
  * Gaming
  * Cloud Services

### Company Exposure Ranking

* Identifies major companies associated with the services used
* Ranks exposure concentration across ecosystems

### Data Collection Matrix

* Estimates categories of data potentially collected
* Includes location, shopping, communication, and behavioral signals

### Risk Radar

* Highlights:

  * Advertising Profiling Risk
  * Cross-Platform Tracking Risk
  * Identity Correlation Risk
  * Data Brokerage Exposure

### Digital Twin Profile

* Separates Facts from Estimates
* Demonstrates how organizations may build behavioral profiles

### Most Valuable Data Assets

* Search history
* Communication metadata
* Shopping behavior
* Social graph
* Location patterns

### Privacy Improvement Simulator

* Shows potential privacy score improvements from:

  * Enabling 2FA
  * Permission audits
  * Tracking reduction
  * Privacy-focused tools

### Final Verdict

Provides an overall privacy assessment based solely on the supplied dataset.

## Technologies Used

* HTML5
* CSS3
* Claude AI

## Key Learnings

* Digital footprints extend across multiple ecosystems.
* Data concentration increases profiling capabilities.
* Privacy can be improved through simple configuration changes.
* Separating Facts from Estimates is essential in responsible analytics.
* Visual dashboards improve understanding of cybersecurity concepts.

## Disclaimer

This project does not claim access to any private databases. All behavioral insights are estimates derived solely from the provided dataset.
