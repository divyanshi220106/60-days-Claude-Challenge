# 🚀 Day 4/60 – Chain of Thought Prompting

## 📌 Overview

This project is part of the **60 Days Claude AI Challenge**.

For Day 4, I explored **Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Prompting**, a prompting technique that encourages AI to reason step-by-step before generating a response. Using this method, I created a personalized **60-Day Career Roadmap** focused on becoming a **Full Stack Developer**, exploring **AI Engineering**, and ultimately securing a **Full-Time SDE Role**.
