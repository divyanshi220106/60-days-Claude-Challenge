# Day 24 – Business Strategy Report Challenge

## Overview

Today I completed Day 24 of my AI Startup Challenge where I transformed my Customer & MVP Blueprint into a complete Business Strategy Report.

The goal was not just to validate the idea, but to determine whether it can become a sustainable and scalable business.

---

# Startup Summary

### Problem

Job seekers struggle to create ATS-friendly resumes that attract recruiters and increase interview opportunities.

### Solution

ResumeBoost AI helps users generate AI-powered resumes, cover letters, and ATS optimization reports in minutes.

### Target Customers

* Students
* Fresh graduates
* Working professionals
* Career switchers

### Value Proposition

Generate ATS-optimized resumes that improve interview conversion rates.

### Revenue Model

* Freemium SaaS
* Monthly subscriptions
* Premium resume reviews
* Career coaching partnerships

### Go-To-Market Strategy

* SEO-driven growth
* Content marketing
* LinkedIn distribution
* Referral loops
* University partnerships

---

# Key Business Insights

## Business Reality Check

### Who Pays?

Job seekers willing to improve their chances of landing interviews.

### Why Do They Pay?

To increase visibility in recruiter ATS systems and save time.

### Growth Risks

* High competition
* SEO dependency
* Low switching costs

### Monetization Risks

* Users may not upgrade
* Price sensitivity
* Churn after first use

### Assumptions To Validate

* Willingness to pay
* Retention after first month
* Referral potential

* Revenue matters more than vanity metrics.
