# 🏥 Hospital Admission Readiness Simulator

> A single-file healthcare workflow training tool for Hospital Admission Coordinators — built with HTML, Tailwind CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript.

---

## Overview

The **Hospital Admission Readiness Simulator** is an interactive, browser-based training application that places the user in the role of a **Hospital Admission Coordinator**. It simulates the full admission readiness workflow — from prior authorization management to bed assignment, clinical documentation, consent, and final admission decision — across five real-world diagnosis scenarios.

All provider, payer, and physician names are **illustrative training data only**.

---

## Features

### Setup & Configuration
- Input fields for Provider/Facility, Attending Physician, and Payer/Insurance Plan
- Diagnosis selector: Acute MI · CHF · Pneumonia · Elective Surgery · Hip Fracture
- Admission Type selector: Inpatient · Observation · Emergency · ICU · Same-Day Surgery
- Prior Authorization (PA) Status: Approved · Pending · Denied · Not Required
- Admission Date picker

**Dynamic notices:**
- **Observation Status** → CMS 2-Midnight Rule notice (cost-sharing, SNF eligibility, MOON notification)
- **Acute MI / CHF** → InterQual/Milliman criteria alert for UR documentation requirements

---

### Readiness Score Engine

Weighted scoring across six admission components:

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Prior Authorization | 25% |
| Clinical Documentation | 20% |
| Physician Orders | 20% |
| Insurance Verification | 15% |
| Patient Consent | 10% |
| Bed Assignment | 10% |

- Initial score range: **30–60%** (final decision withheld)
- **Special rule:** Denied PA + ICU admission type cannot reach 70% through administrative actions alone — PA appeal is required

---

### Prior Authorization Branch Logic

The simulator branches dynamically based on PA status:

| PA Status | Available Actions |
|---|---|
| **Approved** | Proceed with workflow |
| **Not Required** | Proceed with workflow |
| **Pending** | Follow Up with Payer · Upload Clinical Docs · Contact Physician |
| **Denied** | Review Denial Reason · Contact Insurance · Submit Appeal |

A successfully completed appeal **converts PA status to Approved** and restores full PA score weight.

---

### Workflow Actions

Seven actionable steps that each improve score components and reduce risk:

1. 🛏 **Assign Bed** — coordinate with bed board
2. ✅ **Verify Insurance** — confirm eligibility and benefits
3. 📄 **Upload Documentation** — H&P, labs, orders to EMR
4. ✍ **Complete Consent** — patient consent forms
5. 👨‍⚕️ **Contact Physician** — admission orders from attending
6. 🔔 **Notify Nursing** — alert unit staff
7. 🚑 **Prepare Patient Arrival** — transport and registration

---

### Risk Tracking

Four real-time risk indicators (0–100%) that decrease as workflow actions are completed:

- **Documentation Risk** — elevated for Acute MI and CHF
- **Insurance Risk** — elevated for Denied/Pending PA status
- **Bed Risk** — elevated for ICU admission type
- **Clinical Risk** — weighted higher for Acute MI, CHF, and ICU

---

### Admission Timeline

Nine-step milestone tracker that advances as actions are completed:

`PA Review → Insurance Verification → Bed Assignment → Documentation → Consent → Patient Arrival → Registration → Clinical Assessment → Admission Complete`

---

### Care Coordination Team Cards

Five role cards representing the full interdisciplinary team:

- **Attending Physician** — orders and medical necessity
- **Case Manager** — LOS, discharge planning, InterQual/Milliman review
- **Charge Nurse** — unit preparation and nursing assessment
- **Utilization Review** — concurrent review, denial risk, InterQual/Milliman, payer communication
- **Discharge Planner** — post-acute referrals, SNF/home health, readmission risk

---

### Governance Snapshot

Appears automatically when readiness score reaches **≥ 75%**:

| Benchmark | Estimate |
|---|---|
| PA Turnaround Time | 3–5 days |
| Inpatient Denial Rate (CMS) | 8–10% |
| PA Rework Cost per Transaction (CAQH) | $11 |

> All figures are industry estimates for training purposes only. Sources: CMS, CAQH CORE, AHIMA.

---

### Final Admission Decision

| Score | Decision |
|---|---|
| **≥ 90%** | ✅ **ADMIT** — Full admission summary with all details |
| **< 90%** | ⚠ **NOT READY** — Missing items listed, required actions highlighted |
| **Denied PA + ICU** | ⛔ **BLOCKED** — Cannot proceed without PA appeal resolution |

---

## Usage

1. Open `hospital-admission-simulator.html` in any modern browser — no server required
2. Fill in provider, physician, payer, diagnosis, admission type, PA status, and date
3. Click **🏥 Analyze Admission Readiness**
4. Work through PA branch actions and workflow steps to improve readiness score
5. Reach ≥ 75% to unlock the Governance Snapshot
6. Reach ≥ 90% to reveal the final admission decision
7. Click **↩ Restart** to test a different scenario
